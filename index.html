To My 24/7 Happiness & Headache

I wrote these from the bottom of my heart

Sometimes I wonder if you truly realize how thankful I am for you. Because out of all the people in this world, somehow life led me to you, someone who feels like warmth after a long day, like peace in the middle of noise, like home in a way I can’t fully explain.

Thank you for loving me.

Thank you for choosing to stay beside someone who still has flaws, fears, overthinking, and days where everything feels messy. Thank you for seeing me beyond all of that and deciding I’m still worth loving anyway. I think that’s one of the softest things anyone has ever done for me.

And I’m sorry too.

Sorry for every moment my words hurt you more than I intended, for the times my pisedd off became distance, and for every little mistake that may have left a weight on your heart. If I could, I would take every painful feeling I ever caused you and turn it into something gentler. You deserve a love that feels safe, never confusing.

But despite everything, I hope we keep finding our way back to each other. Not as perfect people, but as two people willing to understand, forgive, learn, and grow together. I hope our love continues to feel like something we protect instead of something we slowly lose.

I want us to survive the bad moods, the misunderstandings, the exhausting days, and every version of life that tries to pull us apart. I want us to become the kind of love that stays warm even after time passes.

And if someday you ever doubt how much you mean to me, I hope you reread this and remember that being loved by you has changed me in the most beautiful way possible.

Loving you always, Cp🐾
